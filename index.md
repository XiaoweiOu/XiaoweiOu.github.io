I am a Ph.D. candidate in Physics at Yale University, advised by
Prof. Vidvuds Ozoliņš.
I develop neural-network quantum states and variational Monte Carlo methods
for strongly correlated quantum systems: frustrated magnets, the Hubbard model,
and electron–phonon coupled systems. Much of my work is about optimization,
making large neural-network wavefunctions train reliably when the target
has a complicated sign structure.
